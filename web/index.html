APEX-1 · HOW THIS WORKS

This is APEX-1 — Litoral Systems' cockpit display for a sounding rocket. It shows live or recorded flight telemetry: altitude, velocity, acceleration, air pressure, temperature, and a synthesized attitude view.

The four modes

● LIVELIVEReal data from the rocket or the PC simulator.
REPLAY · CAPTURED FLIGHTREPLAYPlaying a captured flight file. This is what the public page shows by default.
DEMO · SYNTHETIC TELEMETRYDEMOSynthetic flight generated in your browser when nothing else is connected.
● RECRECYou are capturing whatever is on screen (the button glows red).

Recording a flight

Press ● REC to start saving the telemetry you're watching. Press again to stop — your browser downloads a mission file (.ndjson, one telemetry frame per line). Open it later by pressing ▶ PLAY with that file selected. Files recorded in the browser save to your Downloads folder; to publish one for everyone, add it to the repository's web/missions/ folder and push.

Where the data lives

  • Your Downloads — anything recorded in the browser.
  • missions/ on your PC — captured by tools/record.py.
  • data/*.csv on your PC — the ground-truth flight log the simulator writes itself.
  • web/missions/ — what the public website plays.

URL shortcuts

?demo=1 forces the synthetic demo · ?replay=1 forces replay without a live link.
